The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in the directory writes the day first. A date like 7/4 comes out as 4 July instead of 7 April. This hits the app, email and the booking-v2 form. It is what #4471 says, and the queued fix is for this.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, while the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. So people do what the hint says, and the booking lands a month off. Only tenants on booking-v3 get this, and only through the form. The parser fix does not touch it. The fix is the hint text, or the form reading what its hint asks for.

one-day: bookings move by one day, not by a month. These reports do not describe a day and month swapped: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json), but causes.ts files every report by its tenant, so they were counted under parser. A one-day shift between places points at time zones (the invite’s time converted in the wrong zone), not at the parser. The parser fix will not close it.

Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships. The hint reports stay open until the booking-v3 hint and the form agree, and the one-day reports until invites keep their day across time zones.

How these were counted

  • By id, once. The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Each report counts once (uniqueById).
  • By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser at the moment it comes in, and that is a guess. The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts, locales.ts). The tags and the causes disagree in both groups. The #4471 page shows the tag counts.
  • By what the report describes, where the desk read it. The 5 reports in desk-notes.json describe a one-day move, so they count under one-day, not under their tenant’s cause.
  • Not by subject. Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and the problems share subjects (“Booking on the wrong date”, “Date is wrong”). That is how they ended up in one ticket. It saw this coming: “engineering merges them later”. This page is that split.

What the desk can do next

  • Triage new date reports from booking-v3 form users to the hint ticket, and reports of a one-day move to the one-day ticket, not #4471.
  • Consider a decision record that replaces 0001 for incidents: merge on cause (tenant, channel, form), not on subject. The triage explorer already shows pairs with the same subject and different causes.
