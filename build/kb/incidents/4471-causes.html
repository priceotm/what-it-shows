The two problems

1 · parser: the shared parser reads dates month first → what-it-shows#1. Every tenant in the directory writes the day first, so a date like 7/4 comes out as 4 July instead of 7 April. It hits the app, email and the booking-v2 form. This is what #4471 says, and the queued fix is for it.

2 · hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first → what-it-shows#2. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, but the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, and the booking lands a month off. Only tenants on booking-v3 get it, and only through the form. The table above already marks it: not this ticket, no ticket of its own. Now it has one.

The note under the table counts reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). They are filed under parser by tenant and belong to what-it-shows#1; if the parser fix does not stop them, they need a closer look.

Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships. The hint reports stay open until the booking-v3 hint and the form agree.
